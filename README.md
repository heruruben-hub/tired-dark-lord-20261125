Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/competitions/u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb) competition **"The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue"**.

## What it does

`main.ts` writes a single monologue of exactly 100 words (557 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`. The speaker is a fantasy villain — a dark lord in the middle of a siege, four hundred years into a grudge against his own brother — who is secretly just very tired. The tiredness lives in the voice, the rhythm and the small deflections (a lost train of thought, a shrug at his own schedule, a plea to be surprised), never in a plain statement that he is weary. He still has a scheme — the poisoned river, the siege engines, the curse — and a history. The sting lands in the last line.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts   # requires Node 22.18+
```
